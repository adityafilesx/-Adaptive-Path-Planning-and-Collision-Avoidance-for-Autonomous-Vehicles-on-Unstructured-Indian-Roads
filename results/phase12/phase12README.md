## Required 33-point audit

1. **Architecture:** one renderer shared by LIVE observer callbacks and verified
   recorded-frame REPLAY/EXPORT. No duplicated driving loop.
2. **New files:** `mainPhase12.m`, `tests/testPhase12Demo.m`, and ten `demo/*.m`
   helpers: `demoConfig`, `loadDemoData`, `captureDemoData`, `createDemoFigure`,
   `formatDemoState`, `updateDemoFrame`, `buildDemoEvents`, `selectDemoScreenshots`,
   `exportDemoEvidence`, and `runPhase12Demo`; module README and this output index.
3. **Modified source:** `control/runClosedLoopSimulation.m` gained an opt-in,
   read-only frame recorder/observer. Existing workspace cleanup and commits
   were left intact. All 26 Phase 11 files retained at resumption were verified
   checksum-identical; requested regression added fresh timestamped results.
4. **Core algorithm changes:** none. Risk equations, thresholds, prediction,
   perception, planning, steering, speed control, and scenario definitions frozen.
5. **Primary:** Sudden Cattle Crossing, actor 701.
6. **Secondary:** Confidence Drop, pedestrian 801; full replay reached the goal
   in 15.6 s, no collision, five replans.
7. **Layout:** environment left, governor and actor explanation right, risk,
   actual/desired speed and recent events along the bottom; single main window.
8. **Displayed values:** simulation time, total risk, governor state, safety and
   speed scales, urgency, dominant actor, confidence, distance, CPA distance/time,
   actor risk, adaptive scale, actual/desired/nominal speed, path safety, outcome.
9. **Envelopes:** current-time physical-plus-uncertainty footprints multiplied by
   the logged combined adaptive scale; translucent amber, expanding/contracting.
10. **Predictions:** recorded predicted positions as dashed trajectories/points;
    separate from current actor truth and actual physical footprints/yaw.
11. **Active path:** stored vertices of the currently active plan; replaced on
    actual updates. Only the travelled trail persists across replans.
12. **Replanning:** temporary 0.8-s simulation-time indicator with logged reasons;
    complete reasons retained in the event CSV. Initial planning is also an event,
    whereas the reported eight replans exclude the initial plan.
13. **Risk timeline:** recorded timestamps/risks, transition markers and 0.35/0.70
    entry thresholds; no future portion of the trace displayed early.
14. **Speed timeline:** physical and desired speed plotted independently; a STOP
    state or zero target is not depicted as instantaneous physical stopping.
15. **Dominant explanation:** logged maximum-risk actor ID, class, confidence,
    physical center distance, CPA, actor risk and effective envelope scale.
16. **Advanced mode:** risk contributions, class weight and raw risk; optional
    saved planner latency and explicitly labeled Phase 11 global timing means.
17. **LIVE:** tested using real simulator callbacks; histories, frame logs and
    metrics exactly matched the source run. Rendering time can slow wall time.
18. **REPLAY:** passed end-to-end for both scenarios. First use with a new source
    captures missing paths/predictions in an equality-checked rerun; cached replay
    does not run planning or recalculate risk.
19. **EXPORT:** MATLAB-native MPEG-4, PNG screenshots and PNG/vector-PDF figures;
    Motion JPEG AVI fallback when MPEG-4 is not supported.
20. **Video:** cattle export at 10 fps, 366 frames, 36.6 s. The 17.8-s simulation
    plays at 0.5x, followed by one second holding the final outcome. VideoReader
    decoding and timing checks passed; frames repeat without invented states.
21. **Screenshots:** seven real events: A 0.0 s NORMAL; B 2.4 s rising risk;
    C 6.2 s CAUTIOUS; D 13.2 s peak envelope/CONSERVATIVE_STOP; E 8.2 s lateral
    replan; F 14.2 s NORMAL recovery; G 17.8 s goal. Letter order groups concepts,
    not chronology; actual times are embedded and recorded in the summary.
22. **Before/after:** original and 8.2-s updated planned paths, travelled prefix,
    dominant cattle and adaptive envelope. Static analytical axes are individually
    scaled; the moving environment uses equal spatial scale.
23. **Baseline comparison:** paired Phase 11 cattle evidence. ACARG reaches goal;
    fixed margin safely terminates, not a collision. Minimum actor-center distances
    12.13 versus 6.37 m; eight versus seven replans, with trajectories and speeds.
    These center distances are not claimed as physical body-clearance distances.
24. **Confidence figure:** Phase 11 confidence 0.95 to 0.40; uncertainty 0.005 to
    0.125; risk 0.551 to 0.683; adaptive scale approximately 2.191 to 2.429. All four
    controlled cases remain CAUTIOUS at speed scale 0.65; no STOP fabricated.
25. **CPA figure:** actual controlled definitions and saved scores. Moving-away
    actor at 10 m has risk 0.190; collision-course actor at 18.974 m has risk 0.412,
    versus 0.117 without CPA. This is an ablation graphic, not cattle replay data.
26. **Goal:** cattle goal reached at 17.8 s; final display holds when interactive.
27. **Collision:** none observed in the completed cattle/secondary runs. Earlier
    frames say collision observed, not a claim of future collision-free motion.
28. **Fidelity:** exact original logs/metrics/history equality verified before
    accepting additional presentation fields; displayed-state checks cover every
    cattle frame. Existing Phase 11 evidence is never rewritten by the demo.
29. **Phase 12 suite:** 35 passed, zero failed, including LIVE, replay fidelity,
    actor/ego rendering, paths, events, timeline values, export and provenance.
30. **Full regression:** all 11 requested suites passed. Phase 10: 28 independent
    checks passed; 12 RoadRunner runtime tests skipped on macOS. `mainPhase6`
    through `mainPhase11` completed. The visible `mainPhase12` replay was interrupted
    by figure closure; the same entry point completed with `visible='off'`.
31. **mainPhase12:** replay, LIVE test and export completed; Confidence Drop replay
    also completed. Early window closure intentionally cancels with a clear error.
32. **RoadRunner remaining:** genuine Windows/Linux runtime and scene validation,
    still pending; no runtime or realistic RoadRunner footage claimed here.
33. **Limitations:** deterministic simulation evidence, not real-world safety
    certification; schematic road strip is not a drivable-boundary constraint;
    displayed envelope is the current layer, not the future occupancy union.
    Runtime timings depend on the selected Phase 11 source/hardware. The initial
    disk-full attempt is retained in the historical log, not counted as a pass.
